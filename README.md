
# About

## Your Journey to Graduate School
The Graduate Prep Roadmap App is an interactive platform for setting goals and planning ahead on your road to entering graduate school. This tool is brought to you by the Center for the Advancement of Students and Alumni (CASA) at The Graduate School at Georgia State University.

The Roadmap App has a series of recommended activities and events to prepare for graduate school, including:

- Developing essential skills for graduate school
- Securing academic and professional references
- Developing and submitting your applications
- Researching potential schools and programs
- Preparing for entrance exams like the GRE

# Use

## Flexible Preparation to Meet Your Needs
The Roadmap App is a flexible, dynamic tool that allows you to customize recommended activities and add personalized goals based on your needs. Because preparation needs vary by program and each student's stage of progress, the app is designed to meet you where you are. You can take it from there.

## Sidebar Navigation
In the Sidebar, the timeline will automatically adjust when you enter your anticipated start date. Add Recommendations to insert activities based on your preparation needs. Remove and re-add event groups using the Remove Groups button. Add Custom Events allows you to add points and ranges with custom labels and dates.

## Interactive Timeline & Dynamic Events Table
The Timeline is fully interactive and editable. Zoom in and out, click-and-drag items to change their date and duration, or remove them with X. Click-and-hold an item to select more than one. Click-and-drag to move forwards or backwards. The Roadmap Events Table updates dynamically as the Timeline changes. Sort events using the column arrows, and scroll through the full event list. Use the top buttons for different ways to export the table.
